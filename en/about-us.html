About us

A dream became Pekitas

Two brothers-in-law, one shared vision: a place where children feel loved, supported and “at home”.

Our story

In the heart of Kilchberg

As luck would have it, the opportunity arose to take over the premises of a daycare centre. So we decided to open our own: Kita Pekitas at Seestrasse 18 in Kilchberg.

We completely renovated and remodelled the premises and furnished them anew to suit children.

Meet the whole team

The founders

With heart and experience

Juan Miguel Ruiz FernandezFounder · Daycare manager

My name is Juan Miguel. With nine years of experience in childcare, I decided to make my dream come true and found my own daycare.

Over the years I have experienced first-hand how important a loving and nurturing environment is for every child’s development. My vision is a daycare where children are not only cared for but supported individually – a place that awakens their curiosity and unfolds their creative potential.

I warmly invite you to join me and my dedicated team on this exciting journey into the world of education and care. Together, let us lay the foundation for a happy future for your children.

Dominik StutzerFounder · Administration

My name is Dominik and I recently became a father myself. This wonderful experience strengthened my belief that every child deserves a loving, family-like environment. That is why I founded Kita Pekitas together with my brother-in-law.

Our daycare is more than a place of care – it is a home for your little ones, where they feel loved, supported and individually encouraged. A family atmosphere is at the heart of what we do, because I know from my own experience how important a familiar environment is.

Together we create a loving community in which every child has the best conditions for healthy development. I warmly invite you to become part of our family.
